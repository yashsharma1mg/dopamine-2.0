# Stepper — implementation specification

Status: ready

Figma: [Stepper section](https://www.figma.com/design/BsQQUym4xOYfOs419MpBBX/Components-%7C-Dopamine-2.0?node-id=4023-1475&t=2b3pGoo58LPx32e2-4).

The Stepper has filled and outline variants in medium and large sizes. Quantity `0` renders the Add action; a positive quantity renders decrease, quantity output, and increase actions. Out-of-stock disables all quantity actions.
